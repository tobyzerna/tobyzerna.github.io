What aspect of the chosen visualisation first grabbed your attention? Explain the feature in terms of preattentive processing (colour, size, position, orientation, etc.) and why it draws the eye before conscious analysis.

Gestalt Laws

Explain one Gestalt law (e.g. proximity, similarity, enclosure, continuity) that the chosen visualisation demonstrates, and how it helps the viewer group or interpret the data.

Visual Comparison Accuracy
